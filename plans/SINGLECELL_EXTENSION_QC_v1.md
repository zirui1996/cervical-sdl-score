# Targeted single-cell source analysis

Fixed before reading GSE208653 count matrices on 2026-10-29. Use every downloaded sample, identify pathology from exact file names (including ADC_6), and preserve donor as the independent unit. Cell QC: detected genes >=200 and <=7,500, mitochondrial count fraction <=20%, positive total counts. These 10x gene-count matrices already contain selected cellular barcodes; do not claim to have rerun raw empty-droplet calling or doublet removal.

Use broad, explicitly marker-defined compartments rather than an unsupervised malignant-state discovery: epithelial, immune, fibroblast, endothelial, smooth-muscle/pericyte, mast, erythroid, otherwise unassigned. Score fixed canonical markers using mean log1p(10,000-normalized counts). Require at least two detected markers for the assigned compartment, top mean marker score >=0.3 and a top-versus-second margin >=0.15. Cells failing those criteria remain unassigned/ambiguous. Review donor-by-compartment marker summaries, including PTPRC and epithelial keratins, before interpreting epithelial localization. Epithelial does not mean malignant. Do not use S100A8/S100A9 as specific neutrophil markers.

For every donor-by-compartment group, sum counts over all retained cells to form pseudobulk and compute logCPM, frozen Composite and fixed joint program. Report program only when >=30 compartment cells and >=8 of 10 target symbols are measured. Keep gene counts, selected-gene detection and mean normalized expression separately. All summaries are specimen-level and descriptive. With two HSIL and two SCC specimens, do not run cell-wise differential tests, create a false n from cells, claim longitudinal trajectory or assert a clinically validated mechanism. ADC is a separate context specimen, excluded from the SCC-versus-HSIL contrast. Compare epithelial-only and all-cell summaries descriptively to assess cellular-source compatibility.
